# 17 Themes

Ten distinct visual directions. Pick ONE per project — the brief's style first, then the industry match. **Do not default to the same theme every time**: if two themes fit, choose the less obvious one that still suits the business. Each theme changes fonts, colours, shapes and layout rhythm, not just the colour.

Fonts are Google Fonts. Colours are the 600 brand shade + accent; build the 50-900 scale around the brand.

## Colour psychology by niche
Pick the brand colour for the feeling the business must create. An existing brand colour always wins.

| Colour | Makes people feel | Best niches | Avoid for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blue / navy | trust, security, competence | hosting, SaaS, finance, law, healthcare | food (suppresses appetite) |
| Teal | calm, clean, modern care | clinics, wellness, cleantech | luxury fashion |
| Green | growth, freshness, health, money | organic food, finance, eco, real estate (gardens) | nightlife |
| Red / orange-red | energy, urgency, appetite | restaurants, fast food, sales, fitness | finance, healthcare (alarm) |
| Orange / amber | warmth, friendliness, value | cafes, bakeries, kids, e-commerce CTAs | law, luxury |
| Yellow / gold | optimism, attention; gold = premium | accents, CTAs; gold for luxury and hospitality | large text backgrounds (readability) |
| Purple / violet | creativity, imagination, premium tech | agencies, AI, beauty, creative studios | budget / discount brands |
| Pink / blush | care, softness, romance | beauty, boutique, weddings, bakeries | B2B, industrial |
| Black / charcoal | luxury, authority, sophistication | luxury property, fine dining, fashion | kids, healthcare |
| Brown / terracotta | earthy, artisanal, comfort | coffee, handmade, interiors | tech |

Recommended per industry (first = default):
- **Hosting & Technology**: navy + cyan (trust + innovation) · violet + lime (bold AI) · teal + slate (calm reliability)
- **E-commerce & Online Stores**: black + hot red/amber accent (bold urgency) · blush + plum (boutique) · green + cream (natural products)
- **Real Estate & Property**: charcoal + brass (luxury) · navy + gold (trust + premium) · forest green + stone (homes, gardens)
- **Restaurants, Cafes & Hospitality**: terracotta + cream (warm appetite) · black + gold (fine dining) · deep teal + coral (coastal / resort)
- **Corporate, Agencies & Professional Services**: navy + emerald (trust + growth) · ink + loud orange (creative agency) · charcoal + blue (consulting)

## Design references (inspiration only, never copy)
Take patterns and quality bar, never layouts, text, images or brand assets:
- **Awwwards (3D / sites of the day)**: bold typography, smooth scroll-driven reveals, one memorable 3D hero moment, cursor-aware motion (desktop only).
- **wearebrand.io and studio sites**: oversized type, generous negative space, case-study cards with big imagery, confident minimal navigation.
- **Dribbble / Behance**: bento grids, floating UI chips, soft gradients, polished cards and icon tiles.
- **Webflow showcase**: scroll-triggered section transitions, sticky split layouts, interactive navigation.
- **Lovable / modern React sites**: clean shadcn-style components, crisp spacing, accessible forms, subtle borders over heavy shadows.
- **Premium Shopify themes**: product-first grids, sticky add-to-cart, trust badges, quick view, clean collection filters.
- **Professional WordPress themes**: clear service pages, strong hero + trust strip + CTA rhythm, readable long-form content.

## Theme rules
- Glassmorphism only where it helps readability over imagery (navbar on scroll, search bar, floating chips): `bg-white/70 backdrop-blur-md border border-white/40`.
- Gradients: subtle, 2 adjacent hues, never rainbow.
- Dark themes: body text #e5e7eb or lighter, never pure white on pure black for long text.
- An existing brand colour always wins over the theme palette; keep the theme's fonts and shapes.
- **Multiple suggestions**: when asked for options, offer 3 themes from different rows that differ in mood (e.g. T3 Editorial Warm, T4 Dark Luxury, T7 Soft Boutique), each with a one-line reason.
