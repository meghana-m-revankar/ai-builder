About Green Valley Public School

Discover our rich legacy of academic distinction, values-driven education, and passionate leadership in Mysuru.

Our Journey

19 Years of Educational Excellence in Mysuru

Founded in 2005, Green Valley Public School started with a modest vision: to provide high-quality, holistic education rooted in Indian values while preparing students for a globalized world. Located in Vijayanagar 2nd Stage, the school has steadily expanded its campus, faculty, and student body to over 1,800 students.

Affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), we offer comprehensive education from Nursery through Class 12, featuring specialized streams in Science and Commerce at the senior secondary level.

Vision

To create confident, compassionate, and capable global citizens.

Mission

Delivering excellence in academics, sports, arts, and character building.
