Welcome to Green Valley

Empowering Students to Excel Academically and Personally

Founded in 2005, Green Valley Public School has grown into a beacon of holistic education in Mysuru. Located in Vijayanagar 2nd Stage, our campus provides a serene, secure, and stimulating environment designed for modern learning.

Under the visionary leadership of Principal Dr. Lakshmi Prasad, our faculty is dedicated to unlocking every child's true potential through inquiry-based learning, strong moral grounding, and active sports engagement.
