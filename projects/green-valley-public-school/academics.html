CBSE Curriculum

Academic Excellence at Green Valley

Structured learning paths from Nursery to Class 12, featuring specialized Science and Commerce streams.

Programs Offered

Stages of Learning

01

Foundational (Nursery - Class 5)

Play-way learning for early childhood transitioning into foundational literacy, numeracy, environmental science, and creative arts.

  • Activity-based learning
  • English, Kannada, Hindi / Sanskrit
  • Basic Computer & Art classes
02

Middle & Secondary (Class 6 - 10)

Rigorous CBSE syllabus emphasizing critical thinking, scientific inquiry, mathematical aptitude, and social sciences preparation for board exams.

  • Advanced Mathematics & Science Labs
  • Regular internal assessments
  • Class 10 Board Exam preparation
03

Senior Secondary (Class 11 - 12)

Specialized streams equipping students for higher education, professional entrance examinations, and leadership careers.

  • Science: Physics, Chem, Bio/Math
  • Commerce: Accountancy, BS, Economics
  • Career counselling & mentoring
