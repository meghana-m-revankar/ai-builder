Examination & Assessment Policy

Continuous and comprehensive evaluation to track progress without overwhelming students.

Formative Assessments

Weekly quizzes, class participation, practical assignments, and project work conducted through each term to measure day-to-day understanding.

Term Examinations

Mid-term and final examinations adhering strictly to CBSE board guidelines, ensuring students build stamina and precision for board papers.

Remedial Support

Targeted extra coaching sessions and personalized guidance for students needing additional support to meet our high academic standards.
