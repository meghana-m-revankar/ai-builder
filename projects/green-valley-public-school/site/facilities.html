Digital Learning

Smart Classrooms & Technology Infrastructure

Our classrooms are designed to move beyond traditional chalkboards. Integrated with interactive smart boards, high-speed Wi-Fi, and ergonomic seating, we create an immersive environment that fosters active participation and visual learning.

Interactive Boards

Touch-enabled displays for dynamic and engaging lesson delivery.

Ergonomic Design

Thoughtfully spaced seating ensuring posture health and comfort.

Practical Learning

Advanced Laboratories

Empowering students to experiment, code, and articulate fluently with dedicated science, computer, and language labs.

Science Laboratories

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs complying with safety standards, allowing students to conduct experiments hands-on under expert guidance.

Computer Labs

High-performance workstations with high-speed internet connectivity, software programming tools, and regular digital literacy training for all grades.

Language Lab

Advanced audio-visual systems dedicated to phonetic training, vocabulary building, and conversational fluency in multiple global languages.

Knowledge Hub

Extensive Library & Reading Areas

Our library is a quiet sanctuary filled with over 15,000 volumes, academic journals, encyclopedias, and digital subscription databases. Designed with comfortable reading zones, it encourages a lifelong habit of independent reading and research.

  • Over 15,000 fiction and non-fiction titles across genres.
  • Dedicated quiet study carrels and digital catalog kiosks.
  • Regular storytelling sessions and book clubs for young learners.
Physical Well-being

Sports Complex & Outdoor Playground

We believe physical education is just as vital as academics. Our expansive sports grounds promote teamwork, agility, and competitive spirit.

Outdoor Sports Grounds

Spacious fields for football, cricket, athletics tracks, and basketball courts supervised by professional coaches.

Indoor Activity Center

Facilities for table tennis, chess, badminton, and yoga studios to ensure all-weather engagement.

Transport Safety

Our fleet of GPS-enabled buses covers major routes across Mysuru, ensuring safe and punctual pick-up and drop-off services with live tracking for parents. Transport fee is ₹18,000/year.

  • Real-time GPS bus tracking
  • Trained drivers and female attendants on board
  • Strict adherence to safety guidelines

Hygienic Cafeteria

Our school cafeteria serves nutritious, balanced, and delicious vegetarian meals prepared under strict hygienic conditions. Menu items are curated by nutritionists to support growing children.

  • Wholesome, balanced vegetarian menus
  • Regular quality audits and pure drinking water
  • Monitored dining space fostering social habits
