About Our School

A Legacy of Excellence Since 2005

Founded in 2005, Green Valley Public School has dedicated 19 years to shaping confident leaders and lifelong learners in Vijayanagar, Mysuru. Our holistic approach ensures academic rigor paired with creative expression and moral grounding.

"Education is not just about filling a pail, but lighting a fire of curiosity. We welcome your child to our vibrant community."

Dr. Lakshmi Prasad
Principal, Green Valley Public School
