Required Documentation

To ensure a smooth registration process, please keep the following documents ready during final admission submission at our Mysuru campus.

  • Birth Certificate: Original copy along with two attested photocopies for age proof.
  • Transfer Certificate (TC): Required for students transitioning from Class 2 and above.
  • Previous Academic Records: Report cards from the last two completed academic years.
  • Passport Photos: 4 recent passport-size photographs of the student and 2 of each parent.
