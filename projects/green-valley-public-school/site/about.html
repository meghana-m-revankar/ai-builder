Our Heritage

Two Decades of Shaping Young Leaders in Mysuru

Established in 2005 at Vijayanagar 2nd Stage, Green Valley Public School began with a simple vision: to provide world-class education rooted in Indian values. Over the past 19 years, we have grown from a modest institution into a vibrant campus housing over 1,800 students.

Our consistent 95% board results and state-of-the-art facilities reflect our unwavering commitment to academic distinction and holistic student development.
