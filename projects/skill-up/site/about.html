Founder's Story

Built by engineers, driven by data

Skill Up was founded in 2016 by Ananya Rao, a former tech lead who noticed a massive disconnect between creative marketing agencies and rigorous, data-driven execution. Frustrated by vanity metrics, she set out to build an agency focused entirely on ROI and scalable growth.

Operating from our headquarters in Bengaluru, Ananya and her leadership team have scaled over 500+ projects, combining deep technical analytics with creative storytelling to turn startups into category leaders.
