Bridging the Gap From a Small Room to 15,000+ Success Stories

Founded with a simple yet powerful vision, Skill Up started in Ashok Nagar, Bengaluru, recognizing that talent is universal but opportunity is not. Our founder, Ananya Rao, witnessed firsthand how lack of access to digital tools and modern vocational training left capable youth behind.

Over the past 8+ years, we have transformed from a grassroots initiative into a premier skilling institution. By combining affordable courses starting at just ₹299 with rigorous mentorship, we ensure every student gains real-world competence and confidence.

Years of Impact

Our Journey of Growth

Key milestones that shaped Skill Up into Bengaluru's leading vocational training center.

2016

The Inception

Started operations at MG Road with our very first batch of Digital Literacy students, aiming to make technology accessible to all.

2019

Expanding Curriculum

Introduced high-demand technical courses including the Coding Bootcamp and Graphic Design programs to meet industry demands.

2022

The 10,000 Milestone

Celebrated 10,000 successful student placements and established corporate hiring partnerships across Karnataka.

Today

Scaling Impact

Continuing to innovate with comprehensive mentorship, entrepreneurship tracks, and personalized career counseling.

Core Values & Philosophy

The foundational principles that guide our training methodology and community engagement.

Radical Accessibility

We believe financial background should never be a barrier to high-quality education. Our affordable pricing model ensures learning is open to all.

Practical Mentorship

Theory alone isn't enough. Every student is paired with experienced mentors who provide guidance, real-world project feedback, and career insights.

Holistic Growth

Beyond technical skills, we foster leadership, communication, and entrepreneurial mindsets to prepare our youth for lifelong career success.
