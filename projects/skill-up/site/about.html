Founder's Vision

Started by a developer who saw the gap in the industry.

Ananya Rao, a former lead developer turned growth hacker, founded Skill Up in 2016 with a singular mission: to bridge the gap between technical coding prowess and strategic marketing.

Realizing that modern businesses require hybrid talent who can write code and drive user acquisition, she built a curriculum designed for real-world impact. Today, our 95% placement rate stands as a testament to that vision.
