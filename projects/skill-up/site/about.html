Founder Story

Bridging Wall Street Expertise and Retail Traders

Skill Up was founded by Vikram Malhotra, a former hedge fund manager with over two decades of experience navigating global financial markets. Having witnessed firsthand the knowledge gap between institutional giants and individual retail traders, Vikram set out to democratize elite trading methodologies.

Under his visionary leadership, Skill Up has grown from a boutique workshop in Bengaluru into India's premier trading academy, successfully training over 15,000+ students with an exceptional 4.9-star student satisfaction rating.
