Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about changing or rescheduling your booking.

Can I reschedule my counseling appointment?

Yes, you can reschedule up to 24 hours before your scheduled session time without any extra charges by contacting our support team.

Are sessions conducted online or in-person?

You can choose either option. In-person sessions take place at our Ashok Nagar center in Bengaluru, while online sessions are held via secure video call.

What is the refund policy for the ₹299 counseling fee?

If you cancel your appointment at least 48 hours in advance, a full refund will be processed back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.
