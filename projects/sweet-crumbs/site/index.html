Our Story

Turned kitchen passion into a neighborhood favorite.

Founded in 2016 by Ananya Rao, Sweet Crumbs started out of a deep love for traditional baking and comforting aromas. What began as weekend experiments in a home kitchen has now blossomed into Bengaluru's coziest destination for sweet tooths.

Every recipe is crafted from scratch using wholesome ingredients, traditional techniques, and a generous pinch of warmth. We believe every crumb should tell a story of quality and care.
