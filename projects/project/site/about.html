Founder's Story

Rooted in Tradition, Guided by Compassion

Dr. Ananya Rao founded Zenith Wellness with a singular vision: to create a sanctuary of calm amidst the bustling energy of MG Road. With 15 years of experience in holistic healing and Ayurvedic medicine, she envisioned a space where individuals could pause, reset, and heal naturally.

Our philosophy centers on the harmonious integration of mind, body, and spirit. Every therapy we offer, from Hatha Yoga to Ayurvedic massages, is designed to awaken your body's innate capacity to restore balance and vitality.
