Culinary Artistry

Dal Makhani

Slow-cooked for 24 hours to achieve that velvet texture. A timeless North Indian classic perfected over a decade.

Butter Chicken

Tender tandoor-grilled chicken simmered in a rich, buttery tomato gravy. Finished with a touch of fenugreek and cream.

Saffron Biryani

Aromatic long-grain basmati rice infused with premium saffron threads. Served with our signature cucumber raita.
