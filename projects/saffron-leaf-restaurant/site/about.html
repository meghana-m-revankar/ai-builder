A Decade of Culinary Excellence

Founded in 2015 by Chef Arjun Mehra, Saffron Leaf has redefined North Indian dining in the heart of Jayanagar, Bangalore.

Our Journey Since 2015

What started as a humble dream by Chef Arjun Mehra in 2015 has blossomed into a landmark destination. Over the past 10 years, we have served over 80,000 satisfied guests who appreciate the delicate balance of traditional spices and modern plating.

Our commitment remains unchanged: to bring the authentic soul of North Indian cuisine to your table while maintaining a 4.8/5 average rating across all platforms.

Sourcing Integrity

  • Farm-to-Table Freshness

    We source our dairy and seasonal produce from local Karnataka farmers to ensure peak flavor profiles.

  • Authentic Spice Blends

    Our signature masalas are stone-ground in-house, preserving the essential oils that define North Indian cuisine.

Interior Philosophy

Our design marries industrial charcoal tones with the warm, inviting glow of saffron lighting. Every corner is crafted to provide an intimate dining experience, whether for a business lunch or a romantic evening.

Recognition

Awarded "Best North Indian Fine Dining" for three consecutive years by Bangalore Food Reviewers. We are proud of our 10-year legacy of consistency and excellence.
