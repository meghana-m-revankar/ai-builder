Signature Creations

Dal Makhani ₹450

Slow-cooked black lentils simmered for 24 hours with a blend of secret spices. Finished with a dollop of fresh cream and saffron butter.

Butter Chicken ₹650

Tender tandoor-grilled chicken morsels bathed in a velvety tomato-fenugreek reduction. A timeless classic perfected over a decade.

Saffron Biryani ₹550

Aromatic long-grain basmati rice infused with premium Kashmiri saffron. Layered with marinated vegetables and served with artisanal raita.

A Visionary Journey

Founded in 2015 by Chef Arjun Mehra, Saffron Leaf was born from a passion for preserving traditional North Indian techniques while embracing modern plating and sustainability. Our kitchen is powered by four master chefs specializing in tandoor and curries.
