Guidelines

Borrowing Policies & Library Etiquette

To ensure fair access and preserve our collection of 12,000+ volumes for all 1,500+ active members, we enforce clear guidelines during our operating hours (Mon - Sat: 8:30 AM - 4:00 PM).

01

Maximum Book Limit

Every registered member is permitted to borrow a maximum of 3 books simultaneously.

02

Loan Duration

The standard loan period is 14 days, with options to renew once if no reservations exist.

03

Overdue Fines

Late returns incur a nominal fine of ₹5 per day per book to encourage prompt circulation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about borrowing and library access.

Can I renew books if I haven't finished reading them?

Yes, books can be renewed once for an additional 14 days, provided no other member has placed a reservation on the title.

What happens if a borrowed book is lost or damaged?

Members are required to replace the lost/damaged book with a fresh copy or pay the current replacement cost along with any accrued late fines.

Are reference books allowed to be taken home?

No, reference materials and rare Marathi/Hindi manuscripts must be consulted strictly within the library premises during our operational hours.
