Our Journey

A Quarter Century of Turning Pages

Founded in 1998, Central started as a humble reading corner with just a few hundred titles. Over the decades, thanks to the unwavering support of the Sunrise School management and our passionate community, we have grown into a vibrant literary hub housing over 12,000+ books.

Today, we proudly serve over 1,500+ active members and host 40+ annual reading events that ignite curiosity and foster lifelong learning across generations on FC Road, Pune.

Our Core Values

The foundational principles that guide our library's mission within Sunrise School.

Literacy

We champion reading fluency and comprehension as fundamental building blocks for academic and personal success. Every child deserves access to rich, engaging literature.

Community

We unite students, parents, alumni, and faculty in a shared passion for storytelling and knowledge sharing. Our space is a welcoming sanctuary for all book lovers.

Exploration

We encourage inquisitive minds to venture beyond textbooks into fiction, science, history, and regional literature. Curiosity is the true engine of discovery.

Milestones in Our History

Key chapters in the evolution of Central at Sunrise School.

1998

Foundation & Opening

Central was established on the Sunrise School campus on FC Road, Pune, opening its doors with a core collection of 500 books.

2008

Expansion & Digital Cataloging

Surpassed 5,000 volumes and introduced computerized cataloging to streamline student checkouts and inventory management.

2018

Community Membership Open

Extended membership programs to parents and alumni, officially crossing 1,000 active members and launching annual author fest series.

Present Day

A Thriving Literary Hub

Housing over 12,000+ books across multiple genres and hosting 40+ annual reading events, inspiring the next generation of thinkers.
