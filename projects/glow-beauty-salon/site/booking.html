Booking & Cancellation FAQs

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes, you can easily reschedule your appointment up to 24 hours in advance by contacting our reception via phone or WhatsApp.

What is the cancellation policy?

We kindly request at least 24 hours notice for any cancellations. Late cancellations or no-shows may affect future priority bookings.

Do I need to arrive early?

We recommend arriving 10 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment time to check in, unwind, and enjoy a welcome beverage.

Need Immediate Assistance?

Our front desk team is available daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM to assist with bespoke requests and group bookings.
