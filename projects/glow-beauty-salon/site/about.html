The Visionary

Founded on Passion & Heritage by Sarah Al-Maktoum

Since 2016, Sarah Al-Maktoum has been a visionary leader in the Dubai beauty landscape. By blending time-honoured Arabian beauty rituals with cutting-edge global trends, she created a sanctuary where every client experiences personalized luxury.
