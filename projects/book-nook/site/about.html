Founder's Vision

Meena Joshi's Dream for Pune's Youth

Founded in 2010 by Head Librarian Meena Joshi, Book Nook began as a humble reading room on FC Road with a singular mission: to make quality literature accessible and engaging for every young student in Pune.

Recognizing the growing digital distraction among youth, Meena envisioned a structured yet welcoming sanctuary where physical books spark genuine curiosity, critical thinking, and a profound appreciation for storytelling.

Our Journey

A Decade of Literary Growth

From a modest collection to a thriving intellectual hub on FC Road.

2010

Foundation & Doors Open

Book Nook officially opened at 12 FC Road under Meena Joshi, starting with an initial curated collection of foundational children's and young adult literature.

2016

Community Expansion

Crossed the milestone of 250 registered students and integrated systematic cataloging to manage our expanding inventory of academic and fiction titles.

Today

Modern Digital Tracking

Now managing over 15,000 cataloged books and 450+ active students with an efficient 14-day loan system and streamlined digital dashboards.

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

Core Values
