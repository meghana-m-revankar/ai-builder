Head Librarian & Founder

Meena Joshi

Meena Joshi is a passionate educator with a lifelong vision for literacy and youth empowerment. Since founding Book Nook in 2012, she has dedicated her career to making literature accessible and engaging for every student who walks through our doors.

Her leadership guides our core philosophy: that every book issued is a step toward a brighter, more informed tomorrow. Under her guidance, our team of 4 dedicated professionals ensures the library remains a warm, orderly, and inspiring sanctuary.

Our Core Values

The foundational pillars that guide our daily interactions, collection development, and community programs.

Knowledge

We believe accurate, diverse, and well-organized information is the bedrock of academic excellence and personal growth for young minds.

Curiosity

Encouraging students to ask questions, explore unfamiliar genres, and discover independent answers through our extensive reference assistance.

Community

Creating a safe, welcoming, and inclusive reading corner where students collaborate, share stories, and build lifelong bonds over books.

Institutional Milestones

Key highlights of our journey from a small reading room to Pune's trusted student library.

2012

Foundation & Doors Open

Book Nook was founded on FC Road by Meena Joshi with an initial collection of 500 books, aiming to give local students a dedicated study space.

2016

Crossing 2,500 Volumes

Expanded our physical premises and introduced free reference assistance to support growing academic demands from high school and college members.

2020

Digital Cataloging Integration

Upgraded our inventory systems under Priya Sharma to streamline book issuing and maintain our exceptional 98% borrow efficiency rate.

2024

800+ Active Members

Celebrating over 12 years of community service with more than 5,000 cataloged books and a robust team of 4 dedicated library staff members.
