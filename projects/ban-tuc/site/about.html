About Ban tuc

Seamless logistics solutions driving a growing India forward with precision, speed, and unwavering reliability since 2016.

Our Origin

Built to solve urban delivery bottlenecks

Ban tuc was founded in 2016 by Arjun Mehta, a former supply chain analyst who witnessed firsthand the inefficiencies plaguing urban freight and last-mile distribution across India.

Frustrated by fragmented networks and delayed shipments, Arjun set out to build a tech-enabled logistics backbone. Today, we bridge distances effortlessly, connecting businesses to customers with unmatched speed and transparency.

"Logistics isn't just about moving boxes from A to B. It's about empowering businesses to scale without borders." — Arjun Mehta, Founder
