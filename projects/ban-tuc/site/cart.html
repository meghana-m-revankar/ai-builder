Billing & Tracking FAQ

Got questions about invoices, live tracking, or cargo insurance? Find quick answers here.

How do I download my tax invoice?

Once your order is confirmed, a GST-compliant tax invoice is automatically generated and sent to your registered email. You can also download it anytime from your dashboard.

Can I modify pickup details after confirmation?

Yes, you can update pickup instructions up to 2 hours before dispatch by contacting our customer success team at hello@bantuc.in or calling +91 80 4567 8910.

How is live shipment tracking enabled?

As soon as the driver is assigned, an SMS and email containing a live GPS tracking link will be sent to your phone number for real-time updates across India.
