Founder's Vision

From Botanist's Garden to Your Plate

Founded in 2016 by Ananya Rao, a passionate horticulturist turned chef, GreenLeaf Cafe was born out of a deep love for sustainable agriculture and wholesome cooking. Ananya envisioned a space where urban dwellers could escape the concrete jungle and reconnect with nature through mindful eating.

Every ingredient we source tells a story of ethical farming and local craftsmanship. We work directly with certified organic farmers across Karnataka to bring you produce that is fresh, chemical-free, and bursting with natural flavors.

The First Seed

Ananya Rao established GreenLeaf Cafe on MG Road with a modest seating of just five tables.

What We Stand For

Core Values That Guide Us

Every dish we plate and cup we brew is anchored in our unwavering commitment to sustainability and quality.

100% Organic Sourcing

We partner exclusively with verified chemical-free farms to ensure pure ingredients that respect nature's delicate balance.
