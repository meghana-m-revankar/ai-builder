Founder's Vision

Rooted in Balance, Driven by Compassion

Founded in 2016 by Dr. Anjali Sharma, a visionary in integrative health, JioLife was born out of a desire to bridge the gap between ancient healing traditions and modern scientific insights. With over 8+ years of expertise, Dr. Sharma envisioned a sanctuary where individuals could find personalized guidance for their mind, body, and spirit.

Located at our flagship center on MG Road, Bengaluru, we have spent nearly a decade refining our holistic frameworks. We believe that true wellness is not a destination, but a sustainable, balanced lifestyle accessible to everyone.
