Shipping and Returns

We process all orders within 24 hours from our Bengaluru fulfillment center. Standard delivery across India takes 3 to 5 business days, while express shipping options are available at checkout for major metropolitan areas.
We offer a hassle-free 14-day return and exchange policy for all unworn items in their original packaging. Simply log into your account dashboard, select your order, and schedule a free courier pickup from your doorstep.

Payments and Security

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), UPI, net banking across all prominent Indian banks, and popular digital wallets. Cash on delivery (COD) is also available for orders up to ₹5,000.
Absolutely. All transactions are processed through 256-bit SSL encryption via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. We never store your full card details on our servers, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Size Guides and Fit

Every product page features a detailed, item-specific size chart with measurements in inches and centimeters. Our head stylist Priya recommends comparing your personal measurements with our chart for the ideal modern fit.
