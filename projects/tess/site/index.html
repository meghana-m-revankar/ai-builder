Our Story & Purpose

Brewing Hope, Empowering Youth

Established in 2018 by Sunita Deshmukh, TEss was founded on a simple yet powerful belief: that exceptional coffee can be a vehicle for profound social change. We provide rigorous vocational barista and culinary training to underprivileged youth, equipping them with sustainable livelihood skills.

“When you walk into our cafe opposite Fergusson College, you are not just enjoying a cup of coffee; you are investing in the dreams and futures of Pune's next generation.” — Sunita Deshmukh, Founder & Executive Director

The TEss Impact Model

How Your Coffee Funds Vocational Education

We operate on a sustainable social enterprise model. 100% of the surplus generated from our cafe operations goes directly back into running our comprehensive 6-month barista and culinary academy for underprivileged youth.

Skill Training

Professional certification in hospitality, coffee brewing, and kitchen operations.
