Our Story

Where Parisian Charm Meets Bangalore's Warmth

Founded in 2016 by Ananya Rao, MaiSon was born out of a deep passion to bring authentic European cafe culture, specialty coffee, and organic comfort to the bustling heart of MG Road.

The Visionary

Crafting a sanctuary of comfort on MG Road

After spending years studying culinary arts and cafe management in Paris, Ananya Rao returned to Bengaluru with a singular dream: to create an unhurried sanctuary where exceptional specialty coffee meets soulful, artisanal bakes.

Every corner of MaiSon is thoughtfully designed with organic textures, warm ambient lighting, and earthy tones to offer a tranquil escape from the city's fast pace. Whether you are dropping by for a morning Cortado or settling in with a book and a Pistachio Croissant, our doors are always open to warm conversations.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way

From a quiet corner on MG Road to becoming Bengaluru's most cherished European-style cafe.

2016

The Inception

MaiSon opens its doors on the 2nd floor of MG Road, introducing artisanal filter coffees and classic French viennoiserie.

2018

Expanding Palates

Introduced our signature cold brews and organic sourdough sandwiches, quickly becoming a local favorite for weekend brunch.

2021

Community & Craft

Launched weekend barista brewing masterclasses and partnered with local organic farms for our fresh dairy and produce.

2024

15,000+ Guests

Celebrating over eight years of warmth, maintaining a 4.9-star rating while staying true to our artisanal roots.
