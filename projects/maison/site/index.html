Our Philosophy

Where Parisian Elegance Meets Bangalore Warmth

Founded in 2016 by Ananya Rao, MaiSon was born out of a deep passion for slow living and exceptional coffee. We wanted to create a physical sanctuary on MG Road where time slows down and every cup tells a story of careful sourcing and precise brewing.

From our Head Chef Ananya to Lead Barista Vikram, every member of our team is dedicated to crafting moments of pure joy. Whether you are catching up with a dear friend or finding solitude with a book, MaiSon is your home away from home.
