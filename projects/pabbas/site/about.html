Our Roots

Rooted in Pune, Inspired by Tradition & Craft

Founded in the heart of Pune, Pabbas was born out of a simple desire: to create a warm, welcoming space where exceptional coffee meets genuine human connection. Every cup we pour tells a story of careful sourcing, expert roasting, and heartfelt hospitality.

Whether you are dropping by for your morning Artisan Espresso, savoring a Cardamom Cold Brew on a warm afternoon, or catching up with friends over an Avocado Sourdough Toast, Pabbas is your home away from home.
