Meet the Founder

From Passionate Barista to Community Anchor

Cozy Cafe was born in 2016 out of a simple dream by Ananya Rao. Having spent years mastering the craft of espresso and discovering flavor notes across regions, Ananya wanted to create a space in Pune where people could pause, breathe, and savor.

Today, alongside our 6 dedicated staff including expert head chefs and master baristas, Ananya continues to ensure every cup poured and every plate served meets our uncompromising standard of comfort and quality.
