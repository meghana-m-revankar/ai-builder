About Redbus

Transforming transit across Dubai since 2016 with punctuality, comfort, and unmatched reliability for over 50,000 happy passengers.

Our Origins

Modernizing Dubai's Transit

Founded in 2016 by Ahmed Al-Mansoori, Redbus was born out of a vision to streamline urban and intercity travel across the United Arab Emirates. Recognizing the rapid growth of Dubai, Ahmed set out to build a transit network that merges state-of-the-art vehicle technology with uncompromising passenger comfort.

Over the past 8+ years, we have grown from a modest fleet into Dubai's premier bus booking partner. Whether you are commuting daily or embarking on a luxury tour, our commitment remains steadfast: delivering seamless journeys at transparent prices like our popular Intercity Express starting at AED 50.

Our Journey of Growth

Key expansion milestones that shaped Redbus into the leading transport network in the UAE.

2016

Company Inception

Ahmed Al-Mansoori launches Redbus on Sheikh Zayed Road with a small fleet of 5 modern coaches.

2018

Intercity Expansion

Extended regular routes connecting Dubai directly to Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and northern emirates.
