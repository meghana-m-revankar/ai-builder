Our Heritage

Rooted in Passion Since 1996

Founded by V.G. Siddhartha, Cafe Coffee Day was envisioned as a welcoming sanctuary for the youth of India to connect, share ideas, and build relationships over an authentic cup of coffee.

From our very first cafe on MG Road in Bengaluru to becoming a cultural phenomenon across the nation, our commitment to exceptional brew quality and warm hospitality remains unchanged.
