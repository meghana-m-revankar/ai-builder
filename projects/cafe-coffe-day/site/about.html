Our Heritage

The Vision of V.G. Siddhartha

Founded in 1996 by V.G. Siddhartha, Cafe Coffee Day (CCD) was born from a groundbreaking vision: to create a relaxed, comfortable space where Indian youth could connect over a rich cup of espresso. At a time when coffee culture was largely traditional, Siddhartha envisioned a modern sanctuary blending rich coffee traditions with urban energy.

Starting from our iconic first outlet on Brigade Road in Bengaluru, we set out to democratize premium coffee culture across India. We cultivated an atmosphere where first dates, business ideas, college reunions, and peaceful solitude all find a natural home.

Today, with over 28 years of legacy and more than 500 locations nationwide, we remain deeply committed to our founder's original promise—delivering authentic Indian coffee, delectable food, and a warm environment where conversation flows effortlessly.

Our Journey

Milestones Over 28 Years

From a single cyber cafe in Bengaluru to an iconic nationwide coffee movement.

1996

The First Cyber Cafe

Opened India's first Cafe Coffee Day outlet on Brigade Road, Bengaluru, introducing urban youth to fresh espresso alongside internet browsing.

2005

Nationwide Expansion

Expanded rapidly across major Indian metropolises and highway hubs, reaching 300+ cafes and becoming a household name for casual hangouts.

2015

Menu Innovation & Signature Cold Drinks

Introduced iconic signature blends like Devil's Own (₹220) and fresh food pairings, cementing our position as the ultimate hangout spot.

Present

500+ Outlets & 2 Million+ Daily Smiles

Serving over 2 million daily cups with a stellar 4.7★ rating, continuously redefining modern coffee culture for a new generation.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Every cup we serve and space we design is guided by these foundational principles.

Authentic Coffee Sourcing

We source premium coffee beans directly from Western Ghat plantations. Our careful roasting process preserves distinct rich flavors in every brew.

Warm Warmth & Connection

We build inclusive spaces where students, creators, and professionals feel entirely at home to converse, work, or unwind.

Uncompromising Quality

From our Classic Cappuccino (₹160) to Veggie Delight Panini (₹240), every offering is prepared fresh with stringent standards.

Community Hubs

Our outlets across 500+ locations serve as neighborhood meeting spots supporting local gatherings, work sessions, and celebrations.

Craft & Passion

Our expert baristas undergo rigorous training to master micro-foaming, espresso extraction, and personal customer care.

Affordable Luxury

Delivering high-end cafe experiences at accessible prices, ensuring great conversation is always within reach for everyone.
