The Secret Behind Our Brews

Why Our Ingredients Are Special

For over 28 years, we've remained dedicated to serving authentic flavors sourced directly from lush Indian estates.

Shade-Grown Beans

Sourced directly from our own plantations in Chikmagalur, our Arabica and Robusta beans are shade-grown under native trees to develop rich, low-acid profiles.

Farm-Fresh Dairy

We partner exclusively with local dairy co-ops to source pasteurized, whole milk every morning, guaranteeing silky micro-foam for every single cappuccino.

Artisanal Spices

Our signature Masala Chai features hand-crushed green cardamom, fresh ginger, cloves, and cinnamon, creating an invigorating traditional cup.
