Master the Brew at Home

Elevate your morning routine with expert advice straight from Anjali, our Head Barista.

Water Temperature Matters

Always brew your coffee with water just off the boil (around 90°C to 95°C) to extract the rich flavors without burning the delicate beans.

Grind Just Before Brewing

For the ultimate aroma and taste, grind your coffee beans right before preparation. Match your grind size to your brewing method.

The Perfect Ratio

Use a golden ratio of 1:15 to 1:16 coffee-to-water for a balanced cup. Weighing your grounds guarantees consistency every single time.
