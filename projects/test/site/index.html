Our Story

Rooted in Heritage, Elevated by Modernity

Founded by Arjun Reddy, Test was born out of a profound love for traditional Indian and continental flavors. By combining cherished family recipes with cutting-edge culinary techniques, we create an unforgettable dining journey right in the heart of Bengaluru.

Located at 2nd Floor, 14 MG Road, our restaurant offers a warm editorial aesthetic with cream backgrounds and amber accents, providing the perfect ambiance for intimate dinners and grand celebrations alike.
