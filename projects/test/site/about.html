The Visionary

Rooted in Tradition, Elevated by Innovation

Founded by Arjun Reddy, Test was born out of a desire to bridge the gap between soulful family recipes and cutting-edge culinary techniques. Located at 2nd Floor, 14 MG Road, our establishment has grown to become a sanctuary for true food connoisseurs in Bengaluru.

Every dish tells a story of meticulous sourcing, seasonal ingredients, and an unwavering commitment to flavor. Arjun’s journey has redefined modern dining, making every meal an unforgettable sensory experience.
