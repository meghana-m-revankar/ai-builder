Building a Brighter Future Together

"Education is not just about books; it's about giving every child the dignity of a full stomach and a curious mind." — Ananya Sharma, Founder.

Located in the heart of Bengaluru, Asha Foundation works tirelessly to ensure that socio-economic barriers never stand in the way of a child's potential. We provide holistic support ranging from academic tutoring to daily nutrition.

Learn more about our journey →

Our Core Initiatives

Primary Education

Foundational learning support for underprivileged kids. Completely free of cost to ensure 100% accessibility.

Midday Meal Scheme

Nutritionally balanced meals served daily to keep our students healthy and focused during their studies.

Digital Literacy

Essential computer skills training to prepare the next generation for the modern workforce.
