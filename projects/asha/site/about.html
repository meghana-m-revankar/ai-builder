Our Journey of Hope

Since 2016, we have dedicated ourselves to bridging the educational gap for Bengaluru's underprivileged youth, one meal and one lesson at a time.

Built on Compassion

Founded by Ananya Sharma in 2016, Asha Foundation started in a small room on MG Road with a simple goal: ensuring no child is left behind due to hunger or lack of resources. Today, we stand as a beacon of change, proving that education is the ultimate equalizer.

Our mission is to foster self-reliance in children through holistic support, combining nutritional security with quality academic guidance to prepare them for a brighter future.

8 Years of Impact

2016: The Beginning

Asha Foundation is founded in Ashok Nagar, launching our first primary education and midday meal pilot program.

2019: Digital Expansion

Introduced Digital Literacy Workshops to equip our students with modern technical skills for the 21st-century job market.

2024: Scaling New Heights

Surpassed 1.2 million meals served and expanded our vocational training network across the city.

Transparency

Every donation is accounted for with rigorous auditing, ensuring your contributions reach exactly where they are needed most.

Empathy

We treat every child as an individual, recognizing their unique potential and addressing their emotional and nutritional needs.

Excellence

We strive for the highest quality in education, using modern tools and experienced educators to deliver impactful lessons.
