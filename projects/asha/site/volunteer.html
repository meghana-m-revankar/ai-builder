Your Volunteer Journey

Comprehensive Training

We provide all necessary orientation and resources to ensure you feel confident and prepared before your first session.

Supportive Community

Connect with fellow volunteers and our dedicated staff who are all working towards the same mission of educational equity.

Flexible Scheduling

Whether you can spare a few hours a week or a whole weekend, we value every moment of time you contribute to our children.

Available Roles

Classroom Teacher

Assist in delivering high-quality primary education sessions. Help children grasp core concepts in literacy and numeracy.

Meal Server

Be the face of our midday meal scheme. Ensure every child receives a nutritious meal with warmth and care.

After-School Mentor

Provide one-on-one academic support and career guidance to children seeking to improve their future prospects.
