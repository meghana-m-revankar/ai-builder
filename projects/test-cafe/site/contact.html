Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting Test Cafe or booking a table for your next gathering.

Q1 Do I need to make a reservation or can I walk in?

We always welcome walk-ins across our indoor seating and natural wood patio areas! However, for weekend brunch or groups larger than four, we strongly recommend booking a table online in advance.

Q2 Are pets allowed inside Test Cafe?

Yes! We are a pet-friendly cafe. Your furry friends are more than welcome to join you in our outdoor seating areas where water bowls and treats are always ready.

Q3 Do you offer vegan or gluten-free menu items?

Absolutely. Our kitchen lead Priya crafts several options including plant-based milks for your Hazelnut Latte and delicious gluten-free artisanal sandwich alternatives.

Q4 Where is the best place to park near Koregaon Park?

Street parking is available along our lane. Our front of house team Sameer and staff are always happy to help guide you to convenient parking spots when you arrive.
