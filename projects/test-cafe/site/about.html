Rooted in Passion

Started by a coffee enthusiast to bring artisanal roasting to Pune

Founded in 2019 by Ananya Rao, Test Cafe was born out of a simple desire: to introduce Pune to the nuanced world of artisanal coffee roasting and thoughtfully crafted sandwiches. What began as a cozy corner for conversation has blossomed into a beloved community landmark in Koregaon Park.

Every bean we source is ethically harvested and roasted to perfection, highlighting unique flavor profiles. We pair our brews with wholesome, gourmet ingredients, ensuring every visit feels like coming home.
