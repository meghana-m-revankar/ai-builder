Reservation FAQs

Everything you need to know about booking a table with us.

Do I need to reserve a table in advance?

While walk-ins are always welcome, we recommend booking online, especially on weekends, to secure your preferred seating and avoid wait times.

How long will my table be held?

We hold reserved tables for up to 15 minutes past your booking time. If you are running late, please call us at +91 20 2615 0000.

Can I book for large groups or private events?

Yes! For groups larger than 6, or private gatherings, please use our group booking option below or contact us directly via email at hello@testcafe.in.

Is parking available at Koregaon Park?

Street parking is available nearby, and our Front of House team Sameer and staff can guide you upon arrival.
