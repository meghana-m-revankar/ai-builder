Our Heritage

Founded with a passion for exceptional brewing

Since 2019, Test Cafe has been a beloved cornerstone of Koregaon Park. Ananya Rao, a dedicated coffee enthusiast, started this cafe to bring true artisanal roasting and gourmet dining to Pune. Every cup tells a story of careful sourcing and expert craftsmanship.

Alongside our Head Barista Rohan and Kitchen Lead Priya, we pour our hearts into creating a warm, welcoming space where every visitor feels like family.
