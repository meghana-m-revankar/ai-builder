Our Process

How It's Made

A seamless 3-step quality process turning raw farm potatoes into golden perfection.

01

Hand-Picked Sorting

We select only premium quality potatoes of the ideal size and starch content, washing and peeling them meticulously.

02

Precision Slicing & Frying

Sliced to exact thickness and cooked in healthy edible oils at controlled temperatures for the ultimate crisp.

03

Tossing & Sealing

Tossed in our signature spice blends and instantly sealed in airtight nitrogen packs to lock in freshness.
