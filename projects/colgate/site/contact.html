Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding product delivery, order tracking, and our return policies.

How long does shipping take?

Standard delivery across India typically takes 3 to 5 business days depending on your location. Orders placed before 1:00 PM are dispatched on the same day.

What is your return and refund policy?

We offer a 7-day hassle-free return policy for any unopened or damaged products purchased directly through our website. Contact customer support to initiate a return.

Are shipping charges applicable?

We offer free standard shipping on all orders above ₹299. For orders below this threshold, a nominal shipping fee of ₹40 is applied at checkout.

How can I track my product delivery?

Once your order is shipped, you will receive an SMS and email containing the tracking link and courier details to monitor your delivery status in real-time.
