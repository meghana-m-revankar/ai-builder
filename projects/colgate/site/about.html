Founded in 1806

The Vision of William Colgate

In 1806, our founder William Colgate started a starch, soap, and candle business on Dutch Street in New York City. Built on a foundation of integrity, quality, and community care, the enterprise quickly became a household name synonymous with trust.

That pioneering spirit lives on today in Bengaluru and across the world. We continuously strive to advance oral care science, ensuring that every product we create helps people lead healthier, happier lives.
