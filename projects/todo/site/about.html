Our Heritage

Over 8 Years of Redefining Bengaluru Real Estate

Todo was born out of a distinct vision: to bridge the gap between cold transactional real estate and warm, thoughtful interior architecture. Founded in 2016 by Vikram Sethi, our firm started on MG Road with a small team of passionate design enthusiasts and seasoned brokers.

Over the past eight years, we have grown into Bengaluru's premier real estate consultancy. By integrating rigorous structural evaluation with high-end lifestyle curation, we ensure every client finds not just a property, but a true sanctuary tailored to modern living.
