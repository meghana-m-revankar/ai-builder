Founder's Vision

Rajesh Sharma

Founded in 2016, Rajesh envisioned a space where traditional South Indian filter coffee would be elevated through precision roasting and sustainable sourcing. With over 8 years of brewing excellence, he continues to lead our team in perfecting the balance between heritage techniques and modern cafe standards.
