The Soul of South Indian Coffee

From the misty slopes of Chikmagalur to the heart of Bengaluru, we have been perfecting the art of the perfect filter brew since 2016.

Our Journey from Estate to Cup

Founded by Rajesh Sharma, our cafe began with a single mission: to bring the authentic taste of Chikmagalur's coffee estates to the urban landscape of MG Road. We believe that true filter coffee is not just a drink, but a ritual of patience, tradition, and community.

Over the last 8 years, we have scaled our operations while keeping the family-secret roast profile intact. Every cup served at our 14 MG Road location carries the legacy of generations of coffee growers.

Eight Years of Brewing

2016

Founded our first small cafe stall with a vision to revolutionize filter coffee culture.

2018

Expanded our kitchen to include traditional snacks like Ghee Roast Dosa and fresh Idli.

2021

Launched our retail decoction bottles to help customers brew authentic coffee at home.

2024

Celebrated 12,000+ loyal customers and expanded our seating capacity on MG Road.

Authenticity

We source our beans directly from heritage estates in Chikmagalur to ensure a genuine flavor profile.

Tradition

Our decoction process honors the traditional slow-drip method, maintaining the rich, bold essence of coffee.

Community

We believe in creating a welcoming space where ideas flow and friendships are formed over a hot brew.
