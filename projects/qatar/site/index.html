Our Heritage

Inspired by Grandmother's Spice Blends

Founded in 2016 by Chef Amira Al-Farsi, Qatar brings the soulful, aromatic traditions of the Middle East to Ashok Nagar. Every dish tells a generational story of warmth, hospitality, and uncompromising culinary passion.
