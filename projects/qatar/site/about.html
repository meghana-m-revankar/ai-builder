Rooted in Tradition

Authentic Middle Eastern Flavors in the Heart of Bengaluru

Founded in 2016 by Chef Amira Al-Farsi, Qatar was born out of a deep passion for sharing the rich, aromatic culinary heritage of the Middle East. Inspired by her grandmother's treasured spice blends, Chef Amira brought these authentic recipes to MG Road, creating a sanctuary of warmth and luxurious hospitality.

For over 8 years, we have delighted more than 50,000 guests with dishes crafted from the finest ingredients, slow-cooked meats, and hand-ground spices. Every meal here is an invitation to experience genuine Levantine and Gulf hospitality in an elegant editorial setting.
