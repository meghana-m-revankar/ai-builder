Our Genesis

Born from a vision to transform how brands connect online

Founded in 2016 by Ananya Rao at 2nd Floor, 14 MG Road, Ashok Nagar, Bengaluru, Ponds began as a boutique consultancy with a radical belief: that digital marketing should be both wildly creative and ruthlessly accountable.

Over the past 8+ years, we have evolved into a powerhouse agency blending advanced performance marketing, SEO strategy, and high-impact content production. We’ve scaled over 500+ projects from local disruptors into category leaders across India and beyond.
