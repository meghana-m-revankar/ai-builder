Founded in 2012

A vision for accessible, high-quality dental care

SmileCare Dental Clinic was established in 2012 by Dr. Arvind Krishnan with a singular mission: to provide world-class dental treatments in a calm, welcoming, and sterile environment. Located in the heart of Teynampet, Chennai, our practice has grown from a modest clinic into a premier dental destination.

Our philosophy centers on patient-first care. We believe that visiting the dentist should be stress-free, transparent, and empowering. By combining cutting-edge technology with genuine compassion, we ensure every patient leaves with a confident, healthy smile.

Our Journey of Growth

Key milestones that mark our dedication to dental excellence over the past decade.

2012

Foundation of SmileCare

Dr. Arvind Krishnan opened the clinic at Anna Salai, Teynampet, with a vision to redefine dental care standards in Chennai.

2016

Advanced Technology Integration

Upgraded our facility with state-of-the-art digital radiography and painless root canal equipment to enhance patient comfort.

2020

Specialized Care Expansion

2024 & Beyond
