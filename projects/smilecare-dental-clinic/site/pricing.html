Billing FAQs

Got questions about our billing or payment options? Find quick answers here.

Do I have to pay for the initial consultation?

Our standard initial dental check-up is priced at ₹500. This includes a thorough examination by our specialists and a discussion of your treatment options.

Are payment installments available for surgeries like implants?

Yes, major procedures like Dental Implants (₹35,000) and Braces (₹45,000) can be broken down into milestone-based payments or 0% interest EMIs.

What payment methods do you accept at the clinic?

We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), net banking, and cash.
