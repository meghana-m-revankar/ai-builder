Meet The Founder

Brought from Italy, rooted in Bengaluru

Founded by Ananya Rao, a former barista who returned straight from Italy, Mysore Cafe was born out of a deep love for genuine craft. She wanted to introduce authentic pasta-making techniques paired with world-class brews to the bustling streets of Ashok Nagar.

Every dish reflects her journey across European kitchens and her dedication to local, high-quality ingredients. Come share a cup and a plate with us.

Farm to Table

Uncompromising Quality in Every Bite

We source our durum wheat, organic vegetables, and specialty coffee beans from trusted local and international growers. Freshness isn't just a buzzword for us; it's the core foundation of our kitchen.

  • 100% organic semolina flour milled fresh daily for perfect pasta texture.

Artisanal Craft

How We Make Our Pasta

Follow the journey from humble grains to a steaming plate of authentic Italian comfort food.

1

Kneading the Dough

We blend fine semolina flour with farm-fresh eggs, kneading meticulously until a smooth, pliable dough is achieved.

2

Shaping & Cutting

Using traditional bronze dies, our chefs cut the dough into signature shapes designed to hold sauce to perfection.

3

Tossing & Serving

Cooked al dente and immediately tossed in our slow-simmered house sauces for an unforgettable culinary experience.
