Founder's Journey

From Italian Kitchens to MG Road

Mysore Cafe was born from a passion for authentic flavors and a dream to bridge cultures. Founded by Ananya Rao, a former barista who returned from Italy, our cafe brings handcrafted pasta and exquisite artisanal brews to the heart of Bengaluru.

Every dish we serve carries the dedication of traditional Italian techniques combined with locally sourced Indian warmth. We believe in creating a space where every cup tells a story and every meal feels like home.

Ananya Rao

Founder & Master Barista
