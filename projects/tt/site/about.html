Founder's Vision

From the Racetrack to India's Premier Luxury Showroom

Founded in 2016 by Vikram Singh, a former race engineer, TT Motors emerged from a relentless pursuit of mechanical perfection. Having spent years optimizing high-performance track machines, Vikram envisioned bringing true motorsport pedigree directly to discerning collectors and enthusiasts across India.

Located in the heart of Bengaluru at Ashok Nagar, our flagship showroom isn't just a dealership—it's a sanctuary for automotive art. We believe that acquiring an exotic vehicle should be as exhilarating as driving one, backed by uncompromised technical mastery.

Vikram Singh
