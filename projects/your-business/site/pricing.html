Questions?

Everything you need to know about memberships, billing, and studio policies.

Can I cancel or pause my membership anytime?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. Cancel or pause directly in the member app — changes take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. No cancellation fees, no questions asked.

What happens if I don't use all my classes in a month?

Unused classes on the Starter and Pro plans roll over for up to 30 days. Unlimited members have no limits. We want you to get full value from your membership.

Can I switch plans mid-month?

Upgrade anytime — the difference is prorated and you get immediate access to new benefits. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle. Manage it all in the app.

Are there any joining fees or hidden costs?

Zero joining fees. Zero hidden costs. The price you see is the price you pay. Mat and towel rentals are free on Pro and Unlimited; Starter members pay AED 15 per rental.

Do you offer student or corporate discounts?
