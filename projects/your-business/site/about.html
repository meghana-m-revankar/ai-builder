Founder's Story

From corporate burnout to conscious movement

Our founder spent 12 years in high-pressure finance roles across London and Dubai before discovering yoga as a path back to herself. What began as physical therapy for chronic back pain became a complete life transformation.

After completing 500-hour RYT certification in Rishikesh and advanced training in yoga therapy, she returned to Dubai Marina with a vision: a studio that honours yoga's roots while embracing modern biomechanics and nervous system regulation.

"Yoga isn't about touching your toes. It's about what you learn on the way down."
