Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting Retry Check Cafe.

Do I need a reservation to visit?

Walk-ins are always welcome! However, for weekend brunch or large groups, we recommend booking a table in advance through our contact form or phone.

Is high-speed Wi-Fi available for remote work?

Yes! We provide complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi for all our guests. Grab a Cold Brew or Pesto Chicken Panini and settle in for a productive coding or study session.

Do you offer vegan or gluten-free menu options?

We offer plant-based milk alternatives for all our coffees and chais, along with vegetarian and select gluten-free snack adaptations crafted by Chef Meera.

Where is the best place to park nearby?

Paid public parking and valet options are available near MG Road metro station, just a short 3-minute walk from our cafe entrance.
