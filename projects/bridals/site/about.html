Meet Ananya Rao

Founded in 2016, Bridals began with a simple mission: to empower every woman to feel like royalty on her special day. Ananya Rao combined her passion for makeup artistry with a deep understanding of traditional aesthetics to bridge the gap between modern techniques and cultural elegance.

Over the past 8+ years, Ananya has meticulously curated a team of experts who share her dedication to perfection, ensuring that every bride who walks through our doors receives a bespoke transformation that celebrates her unique identity.
