Our Journey to Elegance

Discover the passion, precision, and artistry that define Bridals as Mysuru's premier beauty destination.

From Boutique to Icon

Founded in 2016 by Ananya Rao, Bridals began as a small, intimate studio with a singular vision: to bring world-class bridal artistry to the heart of Mysuru. Over the last 8 years, we have evolved into a sanctuary for luxury beauty, blending traditional techniques with modern aesthetic trends.

Our growth is built on the trust of our clients, transforming every appointment into a personalized experience that celebrates individual beauty and confidence.

Milestones of Excellence

2016

Established as a boutique studio focusing on intimate bridal consultations.

2020

Expanded our service menu to include advanced skin treatments and global hair styling.

2024

Celebrated 15,000+ satisfied clients and maintained a 4.9-star Google rating.
