Frequently Asked Questions

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes, you may reschedule up to 24 hours before your slot by calling +91 80 4567 8910.

Is there a booking deposit?

For bridal services, a 20% advance is required to lock your date. Regular services are pay-on-arrival.

Do you offer home services?

We specialize in salon-based transformations for the best experience, but travel services are available for bridal parties.

What if I'm running late?

We offer a 15-minute grace period. Beyond that, we may need to reschedule to respect other clients' time.
