Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide services in Mysuru?

Yes, while our flagship studio is in Bengaluru, we have successfully served thousands of brides across Mysuru for destination weddings.

What is your booking policy?

We recommend booking at least 3 months in advance for peak wedding seasons. A 25% deposit is required to secure your date.

Are trial sessions available?

Yes, we offer professional trial sessions for our Royal Bridal Makeup package to ensure your look is perfectly curated for your big day.

How many stylists will be at my wedding?

Depending on the size of your bridal party, we can assign a team of up to 4 expert stylists to ensure everything runs on schedule.
