Our Origins

Born Out of a Endless Curiosity for the Open Road

Established in 2016 right in the heart of Bengaluru at Ashok Nagar, My Trip started with a vision to eliminate the friction of planning journeys across India. What began as a small desk operation has transformed into a vibrant ecosystem of seamless travel solutions.

Led by Ravi Kumar and a passionate crew, we combine deep local expertise with modern booking technology. Every itinerary we curate reflects our unwavering commitment to safety, comfort, and authenticity.

Ravi Kumar

Founder & CEO
