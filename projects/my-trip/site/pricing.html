What's Included & Excluded

We believe in complete transparency so you can plan your travel budget with absolute confidence.

Standard Inclusions

  • All verified accommodations as specified in your chosen package itinerary.
  • Professional tour guides and multilingual support representatives during excursions.
  • Complimentary bottled water and basic travel insurance coverage for all travelers.
  • All road tolls, state taxes, and parking fees associated with booked transit.

Standard Exclusions

  • Personal expenses such as laundry, telephone calls, room service, and mini-bar usage.
  • Optional camera or video recording fees levied at specific historical monuments.
  • Airfare or international flights unless explicitly included in a custom itinerary.
  • Tips and gratuities for drivers, hotel staff, and local city guides.
