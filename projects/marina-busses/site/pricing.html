Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on what's included, how payments work, and flexibility options.

What's included in the quoted price?

Every quote lists inclusions line by line: accommodation (category & meal plan), transfers (type & vehicle), guided activities, entry fees, taxes, and 24/7 support. International flights are separate unless requested. Travel insurance is included in Premium & Luxury tiers; optional for Budget.

How do payments and deposits work?

A 25% deposit secures your itinerary and locks pricing. Balance is due 30 days before departure. We accept bank transfer, UPI, major cards, and EMI plans (subject to approval). Luxury tiers offer a 3-installment schedule. All payments are receipted instantly.

Can I change dates or destinations after booking?

Yes — changes are free up to 45 days before travel (subject to availability). Within 45–30 days, a ₹5,000 change fee applies plus any supplier difference. Under 30 days, standard cancellation terms apply. Your trip designer handles all amendments personally.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellation more than 60 days out: full refund minus ₹10,000 admin fee. 60–30 days: 50% refund. Under 30 days: non-refundable, but we issue a 12-month travel credit for the full value. Comprehensive travel insurance (included in Premium/Luxury) covers covered reasons for full reimbursement.

Do you arrange visas, vaccines, or special needs?

We provide visa invitation letters, document checklists, and embassy appointment guidance for all destinations. Vaccine and health advisories are shared per itinerary. For accessibility, dietary, or medical needs — tell us at quote stage; we design the trip around them at no extra design fee.
