Our Story

Where Every Journey Begins With a Promise

Founded on a passion for exploration, Marina Busses was born from the belief that travel should be effortless, elegant, and deeply personal. What started as a boutique service for discerning travellers has grown into a global curator of extraordinary experiences — without ever losing our founding principle: you deserve a journey as unique as you are.

Today, we design domestic and international tours, honeymoon escapes, family holidays, and adventure expeditions — each tailored by specialists who have walked the paths they recommend.

"We don't just book trips. We choreograph memories that last a lifetime."

Core Values

The Compass That Guides Every Decision

Three principles that shape every itinerary, every partnership, and every guest experience.

Authentic Discovery

We go beyond landmarks to reveal the soul of a place — hidden coves, family-run ateliers, sunrise moments no guidebook mentions.

White-Glove Care

From first conversation to final farewell, every detail is anticipated. Your dedicated designer is a message away, 24/7.

Conscious Luxury

We partner with eco-certified properties, support local artisans, and offset every journey's footprint — because luxury shouldn't cost the earth.
