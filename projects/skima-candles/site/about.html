Rooted in Bengaluru

Born from a love for clean burns and ambient light

Skima's began with a simple vision: to create candles that are safe for your home, kind to the planet, and exquisitely designed. Based in the vibrant city of Bengaluru, every batch is thoughtfully mixed, poured, and cured in small quantities.

We reject paraffin and heavy chemical fragrances. Instead, our 100% natural soy wax blends harmoniously with premium aromatic oils to deliver a clean, long-lasting sensory experience.
