Our Founder

Visionary Leadership Rooted in Bengaluru's Landscape

Founded in 2016 by Vikram Malhotra, Elite Realty was born out of a profound passion for urban architecture and personalized real estate advisory. With over 15 years of deep experience in urban planning, Vikram envisioned a brokerage that transcends simple transactions to curate lifestyle transformations.
