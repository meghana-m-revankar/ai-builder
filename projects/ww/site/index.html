Our Story

Rooted in Sustainability & Community

Founded by Sarah Al-Farsi, GreenLeaf Cafe was born from a deep commitment to sustainable living and wholesome nutrition. Located on Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Blvd, we strive to reduce our carbon footprint while serving exquisite flavors.

Every dish, from our signature avocado toast to our cold brew infusions, is meticulously crafted using ethically harvested, local ingredients that honor both your body and the planet.
