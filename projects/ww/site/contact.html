Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about visiting GreenLeaf Cafe.

Do I need to make a reservation in advance?

While walk-ins are always warmly welcomed, we do recommend booking a table during peak weekend brunch hours to secure your preferred seating.

Are your ingredients locally sourced?

Yes! Founded by Sarah Al-Farsi in 2019, our core mission is supporting local organic farmers and sustainable producers across the UAE.

Do you offer vegan and gluten-free options?

Absolutely. Our menu features a wide variety of plant-based, dairy-free, and gluten-free choices, including our popular Avocado Sourdough Toast and Berry Acai Bowl.

Is parking available near the cafe?

Yes, underground parking and valet services are conveniently located nearby along Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Boulevard in Downtown Dubai.
