Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about your upcoming reservation? Find answers below.

Can we book tables for private events?

Yes, GreenLeaf Cafe offers full and partial buyouts for private gatherings, workshops, and corporate breakfasts. Please contact our team directly via email or phone.

What is the maximum group size for online bookings?

Our online reservation system accommodates parties of up to 6 guests. For larger groups, please reach out to us at hello@greenleafcafe.ae so we can curate your seating.

Do you hold tables if we are running late?

We hold reserved tables for up to 15 minutes past the booking time. If you anticipate a delay, kindly give us a quick call at +971 4 555 0123.
