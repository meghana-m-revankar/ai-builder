Our Story

Preserving Heritage Recipes for Modern Diners

Vikram Singh, a culinary enthusiast, started Ww in 2016 to bring forgotten heritage recipes back to modern dining tables. Inspired by centuries-old royal kitchens and regional family archives, Vikram set out on a mission to honor authentic preparation methods without taking shortcuts.

At Ww, philosophy centers around patient craftsmanship—like simmered 24-hour Dal Makhani (₹299) and fragrant slow-cooked Signature Biryani (₹499). Every dish reflects timeless hospitality, inviting guests to slow down and savor deep, complex spice profiles in a warm, cream-accented sanctuary.

Our Journey Through Time

From a modest 6-table eatery on MG Road to Bengaluru’s premier heritage dining destination.

2016

The Doors Open on MG Road

Vikram Singh founded Ww at 2nd Floor, 14 MG Road with just 10 signature recipes, including our beloved Masala Chai (₹99) and Butter Paneer (₹349).

2018

Culinary Recognition & Expansion

Expanded the kitchen to introduce our legendary Tandoori Platter (₹599) and traditional desserts like Saffron Kulfi (₹199), doubling seating capacity.

2021

20,000+ Guest Milestone

Crossed over 20,000 satisfied diners while maintaining a pristine 4.9-star rating, solidifying our reputation for timeless hospitality.

Today

50+ Heritage Recipes Mastered

Now featuring over 50 unique items served six days a week, continuously setting the standard for warm editorial dining and authentic food.

Pillars of Ww Hospitality

The core values that guide our kitchen and guest experiences every single day.

Uncompromised Heritage

We strictly adhere to original slow-cook processes and classical techniques. No artificial shortcuts or pre-made bases are ever allowed in our kitchen.

Timeless Hospitality

Every guest is welcomed like family into our cream-toned editorial sanctuary. We craft memorable dining experiences with genuine warmth.

Artisanal Sourcing

From single-origin saffron to hand-milled spices and fresh local dairy, we select high-grade ingredients to ensure deep, distinct flavors.
