Meet Our Founder

Sarah Al-Farsi's Vision for Sustainable Living

Founded in 2019, GreenLeaf Cafe was born from Sarah Al-Farsi's deep-rooted passion for sustainability and wholesome nutrition. As an advocate for conscious living, Sarah envisioned a space in Downtown Dubai where community and eco-friendly practices intersect seamlessly.

Every detail of our cafe reflects this mission, from our locally sourced organic ingredients to our zero-waste initiatives. We believe that great food and environmental responsibility should always go hand in hand.

Core Values That Guide Us
