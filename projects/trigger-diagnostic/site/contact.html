Frequently Asked Technical Queries

Everything you need to know about our site visits, remote diagnostics, and security protocols.

Do you require physical site visits for server health checks?

Not necessarily. Most server health checks and cloud migration assessments can be securely executed via encrypted remote endpoints. However, on-site physical audits are available across Bengaluru upon request.

How do you secure our infrastructure data during diagnostics?

All diagnostic tools operate under strict zero-trust parameters with end-to-end encryption. Our security analysts adhere to ISO-certified protocols to protect proprietary enterprise data.

What is the typical turnaround time for a complete Network Audit?

A comprehensive network audit typically takes between 48 to 72 hours from initial hook deployment to final reporting. Emergency patches are deployed immediately following preliminary scanning.

Can we customize our service package for multi-cloud environments?

Yes, our systems architects specialize in hybrid and multi-cloud topologies. We tailor assessment frameworks to match your exact AWS, Azure, or GCP infrastructure configurations.
