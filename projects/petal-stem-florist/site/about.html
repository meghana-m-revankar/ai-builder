Our Botanical Journey

Handcrafted floral arrangements rooted in passion, sustainability, and artistic expression since 2016.

Founded by Ananya Rao

Where Science Meets Floral Artistry

Petal & Stem was born in 2016 out of a deep reverence for nature's fleeting beauty. Founder Ananya Rao transitioned from a career as a botanist to a floral artist, bringing a scientific understanding of plant life together with a modern aesthetic vision.

Located in the heart of Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, our studio serves as both a creative workshop and a sanctuary for flower lovers. We believe that every stem tells a story, and our mission is to translate your most precious moments into breathtaking botanical compositions.

Milestones

Our Growth Over the Years

From a modest boutique studio to a cherished regional name in luxury floristry.

2016

The Blooming Beginning

Ananya Rao established Petal & Stem in Jubilee Hills, introducing artisanal European-style arrangements to Hyderabad.

2018

Sustainable Sourcing Initiative

Partnered exclusively with local organic farms and eco-conscious growers to ensure zero chemical residue and maximum freshness.

2021

Corporate & Wedding Expansion

Launched bespoke subscription services for corporate offices and expanded our luxury wedding styling division across Telangana.

Today

Regional Excellence

Celebrating over 15,000 delighted customers while maintaining our boutique dedication to individual craftsmanship.

Our Philosophy

Core Values That Guide Us

We adhere to principles that respect the earth while elevating floral design.

Community Connection
