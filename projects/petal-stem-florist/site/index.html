Founded in 2016 by botanist-turned-artist Ananya Rao, Petal & Stem blossomed from a deep love for plant sciences and aesthetic design. We believe every arrangement should tell a unique, living story.

Located in the heart of Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, our studio curates ethically sourced blooms that transform ordinary spaces into breathtaking botanical sanctuaries.
