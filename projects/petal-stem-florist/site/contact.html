Common Questions

Frequently Asked Inquiries

Do you offer same-day delivery across Hyderabad?

Yes, we provide prompt same-day delivery for all orders placed before 4:00 PM. Our delivery team ensures flowers arrive fresh and pristine.

Can I request a custom bouquet based on specific colors?

Absolutely! Our lead designer Priya and the team love crafting bespoke arrangements tailored to your exact color palette and theme.

How do I care for my Signature Roses or Hydrangeas?

Trim stems at a 45-degree angle every two days, keep them in fresh cold water away from direct sunlight, and enjoy your blooms for weeks.

How do corporate flower subscriptions work?

We provide weekly fresh floral refreshes for offices, hotels, and boutiques starting at ₹5,000/month. Contact us to schedule a trial arrangement.
