Our Story

Crafting Light, Scent, and Serenity

At Glow & Grace Candles, we believe that every home deserves a peaceful sanctuary. Founded in Bengaluru, our studio handcrafts artisanal candles designed to elevate your everyday rituals.

Explore Collection View Gallery

Rooted in Craft & Care

Every Glow & Grace candle is poured with intention, using sustainable ingredients that care for your home and the environment.

100% Soy Wax

Clean-burning, renewable plant-based wax that ensures a longer, soot-free burn for your peaceful evenings.

Botanical Fragrances

Thoughtfully blended essential and premium fragrance oils inspired by nature’s most calming botanicals.

Hand-Poured in Small Batches

Crafted in Bengaluru with meticulous attention to detail, ensuring exceptional quality in every single piece.

The Founder’s Vision
