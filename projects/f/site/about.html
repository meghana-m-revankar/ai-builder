The Visionary

From Race Tracks to Luxury Aesthetics

Founded by Vikram Singh, a former race engineer who pivoted to luxury aesthetics, F was born out of a desire to bring motorsport-grade precision to street and track cars alike. Every vehicle that enters our studio is treated with the exact calibration and analytical rigor once reserved for professional racing machines.

Our commitment goes beyond mere cosmetic enhancement. We understand the complex metallurgy, paint chemistry, and aerodynamic engineering of modern luxury vehicles, ensuring your investment is preserved and elevated to its absolute peak.
