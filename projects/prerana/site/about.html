Our Founder's Journey

Bridging the Gap in Quality Supplementary Education

Ananya Rao started Prerana in 2016 with a singular, powerful vision: to bridge the gap in quality supplementary education for young, inquisitive minds. Recognizing that traditional classrooms often leave room for deeper, conceptual exploration, she set out to create an environment where curiosity is celebrated and academic rigor meets creative problem-solving.

With over 8 years of dedicated experience, Prerana has grown from a humble tutoring initiative into a thriving educational hub. Under Ananya's leadership, we have successfully guided over 5,000 students toward academic excellence, building confidence and critical thinking skills that last a lifetime.
