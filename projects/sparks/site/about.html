Founded by Ananya Rao

Ananya, a fashion enthusiast, launched Sparks to bring affordable luxury to everyday wardrobes. From a tiny studio on Hamdan Street to a thriving boutique, the journey has been driven by passion and a love for stitch perfection.

  • 2020 – First collection of Silk Blend Maxi Dress (₹3,499)
  • 2021 – Expanded to Casual Linen Co‑ord (₹2,199) and Denim Classic Jacket (₹2,499)
  • 2022 – Launched Floral Summer Midi (₹1,899) and Ethnic Embroidered Kurta (₹1,750)
  • 2023 – Introduced Elegant Evening Gown (₹4,500) and grew to 6 dedicated stylists
