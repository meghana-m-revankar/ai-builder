Meet The Founder

From a home kitchen dream to a bustling Pune hub

Star Cafe was born in 2016 out of a simple love for gathering people over exceptional coffee and comforting food. Founded by Ananya Rao, a dedicated food enthusiast who turned her home kitchen dream into reality, our cafe has grown into a warm sanctuary on MG Road.

Over the past 8+ years, we've remained true to our roots. Whether you're craving our Signature Cold Brew (₹149) or a steaming cup of Masala Chai (₹99), every item is crafted with love, quality ingredients, and a desire to serve joy to our 15,000+ annual customers.
